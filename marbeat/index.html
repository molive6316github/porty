How it works

Beats first, physics second.

01

Feed it a song

Upload any audio file, then pick a 15, 30 or 60 second slice on the waveform. Beats are detected automatically.

02

Name your marbles

2–8 marbles, each with a name, colour and a "voice": a sound treatment, or its own audio file (e.g. a different cover).

03

Record & post

Hit Race. The lead marble's voice plays, handoffs land on overtakes. Record it and download the video.

FAQ

Is the outcome random?

It's seeded. Same seed + same song = same race, so what you preview is what you record. Pick a winner to rig it, or reroll the seed.

What's a "voice"?

Either a sound treatment on the main song (telephone, muffled, bright, bass, radio) or an audio file you attach to that marble, like different AI covers of the same song. Files should line up with the main song's timing.

Does it upload my music?

No. Decoding, beat detection and recording all happen locally.

Music rights?

Use audio you have the right to use. That's on you.

Video format?

Whatever your browser's recorder supports: MP4 in recent Chrome/Safari, otherwise WebM.
